About

I am a Master's student in the School of Software & Microelectronics at Peking University (2024–2027), and currently an algorithm intern at StepFun (阶跃星辰), where I work on long-context post-training optimization for large language models.

I am affiliated with the Embodied & Generative Intelligence Lab at Peking University. My research centers on LLM post-training (SFT, RLHF, PPO/GRPO/DPO, RLVR), LLM agents (agentic RL, deep-research & multi-turn dialogue agents), and multimodal / generative learning. I am broadly interested in making models reason, explore, and self-improve reliably under sparse and verifiable rewards.

Previously I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Yunnan University (Rank 1/107, GPA 3.8/4.0), and interned at ByteDance (GenAI) and Meituan on agent post-training and reinforcement learning.
